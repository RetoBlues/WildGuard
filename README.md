# WildGuard

WildGuard is the business we pitch in our COB300 simulated business plan. This repo holds the two digital deliverables for the pitch.

## Deliverables

### 1. Pitch website
**Live site:** https://wildguard-site.vercel.app/

A one-page site that presents the WildGuard business: the problem, our product, the target market, and the financial highlights from our business plan.

Folder: `website/`

### 2. Garden Sensor companion app demo
**Live demo:** https://wildguard-wildguard-app-demo.vercel.app/

A clickable prototype of the companion app that pairs with the WildGuard Garden Sensor. It shows how a customer would connect the sensor and check on their garden.

Folder: `app-demo/`

## Deployment
Each folder is deployed as its own Vercel project, with Root Directory set to `website` or `app-demo`.
